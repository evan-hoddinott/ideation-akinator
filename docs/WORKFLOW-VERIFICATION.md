## Integrated results

The real workflow completed with a 20-page downloaded PDF; the edited token-free workflow completed with a nine-page PDF. Both match their confirmed scope and estimates. The real conflict gate stopped an unsupported immutable-local-audit promise, and editing that feature cleared the conflict. Research is marked partial and retains gaps. A generated estimate alone is not proof of engineering feasibility or a supplier quote.

The real model-output failure, deadline deficiency, feature-description revision and retests are documented in WORKFLOW-IMPLEMENTATION-STATUS.md. The 68.75-second opening recording stopped before the full journey and is not complete-journey motion evidence. Separate named real-time performance recordings and the full browser interaction record provide the scoped evidence.

T11 coverage was narrowed from repeating the entire journey at every size to full desktop journeys plus the responsive/performance matrix. This follows the approved goal's separate requirements for a full workflow and all-size layout verification; it is stated explicitly rather than claiming an unperformed matrix.

## Public acceptance, 2026-09-13

The deployed demo completed from the main menu through preferences, preliminary research, all three questions, concept performance, configuration, finalization, results and a nine-page PDF. Its only browser API calls were score and PDF, both HTTP 200. Refresh returned to the saved results. The public browser recorded no page errors or console messages, no broken images and no horizontal overflow at 1440×900.

A real final-plan retest against the deployed endpoint reused the completed evidence and returned HTTP 200 in 37 seconds. The compact timeline is `4–6 weeks`; the estimate is $0–$30. The restored real project downloaded a 20-page PDF through the public UI. All confirmed feature descriptions, roadmap entries, budgets and timelines match their respective saved plans in `after/public-pdf-checks.json`. The earlier $0–$80 range and later $0–$30 range are separate model recalculations with disclosed assumptions, not supplier quotes.

`public-research-through-final-print-1440.mp4` is a 240-second real-time display capture covering the public briefing, dialogue and answer feedback, Purl/monitor performance, configuration and final printing through pickup. It ends before results. `public-results-and-export-1440.mp4` records the subsequent results/export actions. These are separate segments, not an uninterrupted whole-journey recording. Frame review is retained in `public-motion-contact.jpg`; the full local final-print/results performance has its own verified recording. The final 1920 cursed reveal is captured in `finish-cursed-1920.mp4`.

Authentication used short-lived signed sessions. Anonymous gating and API rejection were verified separately; a new password-entry login and a human listening test were not repeated. Evidence is local and excluded from Git; the release source and verification record are committed.
