## Journey

A new project begins at BBS with the Sage's welcome. Destinations follow committed steps: problem writing → personal web; problem review → GeoCities; tools and exclusions → AOL; research topics → portal; originality and limits → XP; preliminary research → Flash; completed briefing → MySpace; interview → YouTube; concept comparison → forums; feature workshop → mobile social; focused research → algorithmic feed; plan and printing → generation station; completed print → final 404 world.

The journey clock animates a one-second crossing to an authorized destination. It cannot advance a milestone through elapsed time. Fast workflow changes replace unfinished crossings rather than creating a backlog. Menus pause the world; disabled background interactions do not. Reduced motion resolves the same destination immediately. Resume restores the saved chapter. Debug previews remain independent.

The final plan's existence does not unlock the ending. The saved print handoff does. The closing Sage encounter accompanies the 404 world; the score waits while that encounter is open.

## Conversation and controls

Remove the optional top Chat bar. Put concise quips and useful instructions in the main dialogue, with required handoffs for concepts, features, focused work and completion. Clicking dialogue finishes typing or advances a new sentence. Controls appear after speech; field edits do not restart the turn. Explicit read IDs survive reload. Short viewports reclaim the speech area after delivery and provide a reread control.

Keep the frame's appearance. Problem writing and review are separate views, with one related note at a time. Technology selections and search matches have page controls. Originality uses named selections. Budget retains the animated segmented meter but uses presets, exact amounts and increment/decrement buttons. Production has its own page. Additional constraints are optional pairs, with an action to finish without traversing every optional page.

Research findings and long passages use explicit pages; sources open in a separate document dialog. The feature editor shows one feature at a time, retaining placement controls, dependencies and custom editing. Detailed reports, source documents and dedicated long-text/custom-feature editors may scroll. Required RPG response panels must fit without internal scrollbars or clipped actions.
